# Cadence

Cadence sells video courses to students. This glossary covers the language of the platform: who uses it, how courses are bought, and how money is returned after a purchase.

## Language

### People

**Student**:
A person who takes courses on Cadence.
_Avoid_: Learner, User

**Instructor**:
A person who builds and owns courses on Cadence, and sees the Students on them.
_Avoid_: Teacher, Author, Creator

**Platform Admin**:
A member of Cadence staff who runs the platform. Only a Platform Admin decides Refund Requests.
_Avoid_: Admin (on its own), the team, Staff

**Team Admin**:
A person who buys and manages a Team Licence. They may also be a Student, but a Team Licence alone doesn't make them one. A Team Admin has no say over Refund Requests.
_Avoid_: Admin (on its own)

### Purchases

**Purchase**:
A single payment for a course. It is either an Individual Purchase or a Team Licence.
_Avoid_: Order, Transaction

**Individual Purchase**:
A Purchase a student makes for themselves. It gives the buyer the course.

**Team Licence**:
A Purchase a Team Admin makes for others. It grants Seats, not the course itself; the buyer can't watch the course through it.
_Avoid_: Team purchase, Bulk purchase

**Seat**:
One place on a Team Licence that a student can claim to get the course.
_Avoid_: Coupon, Code, Voucher

**Unclaimed Seat**:
A Seat no student has claimed yet.

**Claimed Seat**:
A Seat a student has claimed. It belongs to that student, so a Team Licence with any Claimed Seat can't be refunded.

### Refunds

**Refund Request**:
The buyer's ask, with their reason, to get back what they paid for one specific Purchase. A Platform Admin approves or declines it. One Purchase can have several Refund Requests over time, but only one can be undecided at once.
_Avoid_: Refund (for the ask itself), Cancellation, Reversal, Chargeback

**Open Refund Request**:
A Refund Request no Platform Admin has decided yet. The buyer keeps the course while it is open, and a Team Licence's Unclaimed Seats can't be claimed.
_Avoid_: Pending, Requested, Awaiting review

**Approved Refund Request**:
A Refund Request a Platform Admin has accepted. Approving it ends what the Purchase granted (the buyer's Place on a Course for an Individual Purchase; its Unclaimed Seats, which can then never be claimed, for a Team Licence) and leads to a Refund.

**Declined Refund Request**:
A Refund Request a Platform Admin has turned down. Nothing the Purchase granted is lost, it has no Refund, and the buyer may raise a new Refund Request.

**Refund**:
The money returned to the buyer after their Refund Request is approved. It is always the full amount they paid. A declined Refund Request has no Refund.
_Avoid_: Reversal, Chargeback, Cancellation

**Pending Refund**:
A Refund whose Refund Request has been approved, where the money hasn't been confirmed as sent yet.
_Avoid_: Pending (for a Refund Request awaiting review)

**Issued Refund**:
A Refund where the money has been confirmed as sent back to the buyer.
_Avoid_: Completed, Paid out

**Failed Refund**:
A Refund where the money couldn't be sent. The Refund Request stays approved, because the decision hasn't changed.

**Watch Limit**:
How much of a course a Student can have Watched and still get a Refund. Past it, they've had the course.
_Avoid_: Watch cap, Viewing threshold

### Courses

**Place on a Course**:
A Student's right to take one course. A Student has at most one per course, whether it came from an Individual Purchase or a Claimed Seat, and it doesn't record which.
_Avoid_: Enrollment, Access

**Watched**:
Seconds of video a Student has actually played in a course: the furthest point reached in each lesson, added up. Not lessons opened.
_Avoid_: Viewed, Progress

**Lapsed**:
A Student on an Instructor's roster who no longer has a Place on the Course, for example after an Approved Refund Request.
